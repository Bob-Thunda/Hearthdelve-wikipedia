Changelog

All versions, newest first. The game updates itself when a new version is out.

1.8.1 2026-10-30 — Big updates in several parts

Changed

  • Updates can now come in several parts. The game is growing with the new art, and each file where the game is downloaded from may be at most 100 MB. The game now downloads all the parts, joins them and checks that everything is in order before it updates itself. If you download the game yourself and it comes in parts (.zip.001, .zip.002, ...), download them all and open .zip.001 with 7-Zip.

1.8.0 2026-10-30 — Armour on the body, a new-looking world and stronger talents

Added

  • Armour shows on the hero. All 27 armours, helmets, gloves and boots have their own 3D model, including the named items and the class sets. Crowns sit on top of the hair; helmets and hoods hide it.
  • New hand-made art across the game: 56 new models of everything from torches, chests, altars and houses to the town wall, the stairs down into the deep and the Pit of Doom, 16 new floor and wall surfaces, and new icons and weapon models for the named items and the sets.

Changed

  • Monsters no longer follow your own stats. They measure themselves against an ordinary hero of your class and level. Better gear and more talents therefore make you clearly stronger. Deeper floors and a higher level make the monsters stronger.
  • Every percent talent gives 1 percentage point more per rank (e.g. Sharp edges 5% instead of 4%).
  • Unbreakable, Ice block and Death's denial now give full life again instead of 1 life, and a moment in which nothing can hurt you: 4 seconds for the barbarian, 2 seconds for the mage and the necromancer.
  • Whirlwind is held down: the barbarian keeps whirling as long as the key is held and there is mana (mana per second). The area grows and the cost falls with every rank.
  • Fireball, Meteor and Frost nova hit a larger area with every rank.
  • Chain lightning deals double critical damage.
  • Skeletons get life from the necromancer's mana, more with every rank.

1.7.0 2026-10-29 — Defensive skills, new art and calmer monsters

Added

  • A defensive skill for every class on F, each with its own look. It sits in the class's defensive branch and needs 5 points in that branch:
  • Barbarian, Granite Skin: you take much less damage for a while, as chunks of rock circle around you.
  • Mage, Ice Barrier: a dome of ice swallows the damage; when it shatters, the foes around you are slowed.
  • Hunter, Leaf Cloak: a whirl of leaves makes blows miss, and you run faster.
  • Thief, Shadow Form: you turn to shadow, and almost every blow passes through you for a brief moment.
  • Necromancer, Bone Shield: bones circle around you and take the blows until they are smashed to pieces.
  • New hand-made art: all seven weapons have new 3D models and icons (the bow in the hand is still the old one, because its string moves). Armour, helmets, gloves, boots, jewellery, belts, drinks, mushrooms, materials and collectibles have new icons. The barrels and the flagstone floor are new too. More is coming in the next versions.

Changed

  • Ordinary monsters attack only 5% faster for every floor (was 20%), because there are twice as many of them now. Elite monsters, champions and bosses still get 20% faster per floor.
  • E is now the only "use" key. F is for the defensive skill.

Fixed

  • A monster's ground attack could cause an error if the monster died before the attack landed.

1.6.1 2026-10-28 — Item level follows the hero's level

Changed

  • Found, bought and forged items have the hero's level (or up to 2 below) — no longer about 3 x the floor. New finds therefore always work in full; the rule from 1.6 (an item above the hero's level only works partly) only affects items from before, from other heroes or from the Legacy chest. The floor still decides how rare and good the finds are (magic, rare, legendary), and relics, set pieces and named items also follow the hero.
  • The Workshop forges and reforges to the hero's level (before: the deepest floor), and the merchant sells at the hero's level.

Added

  • Wiki for players (HTML): wiki/index.html with classes and skills, monsters per theme (share, strength, materials), bosses, floors and worlds, random events, all drop chances, rarity by floor, gear and properties, set pieces, named items (and where to find them), relics, recipes (and where to learn them), materials, drinks, mushrooms, collectibles and the town's buildings — with a search box. It is generated automatically from the game's own numbers, so it is always correct.
  • Changelog as HTML: wiki/changelog.html (from this file).
  • The wiki ships in the game's folder (wiki), and the Esc menu has a "Wiki and changelog" button that opens it in the browser.

Tests

  • New test: finds have the hero's level or a little below (never above), and the Workshop and the merchant follow the hero.

1.6.0 2026-10-28 — Gear above the hero's level only works partly

Changed

  • Gear scales with the hero's level: an item with a higher level than the hero only works with a percentage of its strength — the hero's level divided by the item's level. A level 5 hero with a level 20 axe gets 25% of its damage (and of all its properties); at level 10, 50%; from level 20, its full strength. This applies to weapon damage, armour and all properties (the special powers of named items and set bonuses still work in full).
  • The item shows the percentage in the top right corner (orange), and the tooltip explains it: "Level too high: only works with 25% of its strength ... Full strength at level 20."
  • Note: items from a floor have a level of about 3 x the floor, so a hero who is behind in level will notice it on finds from deep floors.

Tests

  • New test: level 5 with a level 20 axe gives 25% of the damage, a level 10 helmet half the armour, and at level 20 the axe works in full; the tooltip says so.

1.5.2 2026-10-28 — Character selection instead of "new game", and attacks that moved the body

Changed

  • "Start a new game" has been removed from the Esc menu (it deleted all heroes, the town and the chest). Instead there is "Choose hero (character selection)", which takes you to the start screen by the campfire, where you choose or create a hero. In the middle of a delve or online, the button is greyed out and tells you why.

Fixed

  • The body slid away from the hero after attacks: the spear's thrust pushed the hips forward every frame without resetting them, so with every thrust the figure moved further away from the hero's real position (the halo, the light and the ring on the ground stayed put). After six thrusts it was 7.5 metres away. Now the body returns to above the feet after every thrust.

Tests

  • New test: after six attacks with each weapon, the body is still above the hero's position (the test failed before the fix: -7.5 m with the spear). The menu no longer has "new game", but "Choose hero".

1.5.1 2026-10-28 — The blood pact on rebirth, and the multiplayer connection

Changed

  • Rebirth breaks the blood pact (as the Well already does): the pact is back at 0, and the town has to sacrifice all over again.

Fixed

  • The right IP address: the host's window showed the first address the computer had — often a virtual network adapter (VirtualBox, Hyper-V/WSL, Docker, VPN) that the others cannot reach. Now the home network's address is shown first.
  • Code in the same house: when an invitation code points to a game that is also in the list on your own network, the game connects to the local address. Before, it tried over the internet, which most routers cannot do (NAT loopback) — so players in the same house could not get in with the code.
  • No useless codes: if your internet connection shares its address with others (CGNAT, two routers in a row), the game no longer makes a code that can never work, but explains that you need to use Tailscale/ZeroTier.
  • Better error messages: different versions now say which ("the host has 1.5.1, you have 1.4.0"), the game list shows the host's version, and a failed connection explains the likely reasons. The event log writes the address and version.
  • 1.5.0 and 1.5.1 can play together (the protocol is unchanged). Older versions must be updated.

Tests

  • New test of the addresses (home network before virtual network adapters, internet vs. home/CGNAT addresses, code in the same house), and that rebirth breaks the blood pact.

1.5.0 2026-10-28 — Pets follow the hero, and they are rats

Changed

  • Pets grow with the hero: the hunter's giant rat, the Shadow Stalker's Hell rats and the necromancer's skeletons get 15% of the hero's life, damage per hit and armour on top of the skill's own numbers. So they never get weaker than before.
  • Rats instead of wolves and dogs: "Wolf call" is now called Rat call (a giant rat), and the Shadow Stalker's "Hellhounds" are now called Hell rats. The figures are rats (brown fur, long tail; the Hell rats charred with flames on their backs). The monsters in Hell are unchanged.

Tests

  • New test: Rat call gives a rat with the skill's life + 15% of the hero's, the skill's damage + 15% of the hero's hit, and 15% of the hero's armour.

1.4.0 2026-10-28 — Faster monsters, elite packs and champions, bigger rooms

Changed

  • Monsters attack 20% faster per floor: 1.2 times as fast on floor 2, twice as fast on floor 6 and up to 4 times as fast (from floor 16 and in Hell/Heaven). The warning before a hit is never shorter than 0.3 seconds, though, so you still have time to dodge.
  • Softer start: monsters grow from half of "the hero + 10%" on floor 1 to full strength from floor 10 (and in Hell, Heaven and the Dragon's Lair). Without it, the test bot died in every delve on floors 1-2.
  • The bosses are tough again: in 1.3 the bosses only had about 50 hits' worth of life (a mistake in my scaling). Now they again have roughly the toughness they had before 1.3 (the hero's life x 6 x 4 — about 200 hits), and the raid bosses follow suit.
  • The rooms are twice as big (twice the floor area: 20 x 20 tiles compared to 14 x 14 before) with twice as many monsters and twice as much decoration, so they are just as full as before. The obstacles stand further out.

Added

  • The floor's extra challenge (about 40% of floors from floor 2, not on treasure floors, in the Well or Lepreconland): either a pack of elite monsters (3-5 of the same kind in one room) or a champion — one giant monster with 8 times the life, double damage, 1.7 times the size and six times the experience. A message tells you when the floor begins. The champion leaves two good items and a pile of gold for each player.

Tests

  • New tests for the attack speed, the challenges (about 40%, the elite pack, the champion and its loot) and the rooms.
  • The balance bot (8 careful delves): level 9, floor 3, 4 deaths (without the softer start: dead on floors 1-2 every time).

Technical

  • Enemy.hero_factor, Enemy.attack_speed_for/recover_time/make_champion, DungeonFloor.challenge_for/_spawn_challenge, numbers in Balance (ENEMY_ATTACK_SPEED_*, ROOM_SIZE, ROOM_MONSTER_MULT, CHALLENGE_*, CHAMPION_*). Spawn messages have "ch"; Net.PROTOCOL = 18.

1.3.0 2026-10-28 — Monsters follow the hero

Changed

  • Monsters are always as strong as the hero + 10%: an ordinary monster has the hero's life, damage per hit and armour plus 10% — on all floors and in all worlds, regardless of the hero's level and gear. If the hero gets stronger, so do the monsters. The floor still decides loot, experience and theme.
  • Each monster type keeps its strength relative to the others (a rat is weaker than a troll), and elite monsters, guardians, bosses and raid bosses keep their extra life and damage on top. The raid bosses: 20 x a typical boss.
  • In co-op, the monsters measure themselves against the team's average (the players send their life, damage and armour).
  • The numbers are in Balance (MONSTER_VS_HERO = 1.1), so they are easy to adjust.

Tests

  • New test: an average monster has about the hero's life and damage + 10%, the same on floor 1 and 40, the armour follows along, elite monsters still have more life, and a stronger hero meets stronger monsters.
  • The balance bot (8 delves, careful) now reaches level 6 and floors 3-4 with 2 deaths (before: level 9-10 and floors 4-5).

1.2.0 2026-10-28 — Rebirth from scratch, Prestige items and prestige badges

Changed

  • Rebirth (prestige) starts the hero COMPLETELY from scratch: level 1, no gold, a new skill tree, no waypoints, the Deep from floor 1, and Hell/the Stairway to Heaven must be opened again (the victories are remembered, so the rebirths are not lost). The town, the shared chest, the materials and the recipes stay — they are shared by all your heroes.
  • The Legacy chest: everything the hero owned (gear, backpack and belt) is moved on rebirth to the hero's own Legacy chest in town (a button in the chest window). It can never get full. The hero cannot use the items themselves, but can pass them on to the shared chest, so your other heroes can use them.
  • Prestige items: all gear that a hero with prestige finds, forges or buys is called "Prestige <number> ...". A hero can ONLY use gear from their own prestige level (a hero without prestige only ordinary gear). Items you cannot use have a red veil in the backpack and the chest, and the tooltip says why. Drinks, mushrooms and collectibles can be used by everyone. Only a relic from the hero's own level calls Natas or the nuns.

Added

  • The prestige badge (PL): a badge with the prestige level inside — Bronze (1), Silver (2), Gold (3), Platinum (4), Diamond (5), Master (6) and Legend (7+). It gets fancier with every level: a notched ring, laurel leaves, a crown of spikes, wings, rays and finally a rainbow border. It is shown above the hero's head (other players see it too), in the corner of every Prestige item and in the rebirth window.
  • Old saved games: a hero who has already been reborn gets their own gear turned into items of their prestige level.

Technical

  • Item.prestige, LootGenerator.finder_prestige(), `GameState.prestige_block/can_use/legacy/_start_over/ add_to_legacy/legacy_to_chest, PrestigeBadge (scripts/art/prestige_badge.gd), LegacyPanel`. PlayerSync sends "pl"; Net.PROTOCOL = 16.

1.1.0 2026-10-28 — Random events, Lepreconland and update notice

Added

  • Random events in the Deep: about every third ordinary floor (from floor 2) has a surprise. It is chosen from the floor's seed, so everyone in co-op gets the same one.
  • The Leprechaun (from floor 3): a little man in green runs away quickly, giggling, straight through the walls. Now and then he stops to laugh at you — that is your chance. If you catch and kill him before he disappears (about 24 seconds), he leaves gold and a rainbow portal to Lepreconland. He does not count towards the rule about slaying 80% of the floor.
  • The old shrine: touch it for a blessing (damage, full life and lantern, or more luck) — or a curse (more damage to you, less oil, or heavy legs).
  • The fairy ring: a ring of glowing mushrooms. Step inside, and the fairies play a trick on you: you become giant or tiny, run like the wind, see the world mirrored or in the colours of the rainbow — or get moved somewhere else entirely.
  • The goblin party: goblins party under coloured lanterns. Smash the party and take a swig from the ale barrel: you get drunk (the world sways), but hit four times as hard.
  • The wishing well: throw gold in (10 x the floor) and make a wish: four times the gold back, a gift, healing and refilled flasks — or a splash and nothing.
  • Lepreconland: one floor behind the leprechaun's portal. Green hills, rainbows over every room, pots of gold, four-leaf clovers and toadstool houses. Clurichauns (who steal gold), elves and Gold Golems — and the Leprechaun King (cloak, crown and a pot of gold) with a rain of gold coins and his lads to help him. The monsters are as strong as the floor the portal was found on. Slay the king for his pot of gold (60 gold x the floor) and loot; the way out leads further down into the Deep, one floor deeper. In co-op, the team votes on going through the portal.
  • Update notice in the game: a small box on the left side shows when a new version is out — even in the middle of a delve. The game checks for a new version every 10 minutes. "Update now" works in town (the game restarts); on a delve it says that you can update when you are home. "Later" hides it until an even newer version comes out.

Technical

  • RandomEvents (scripts/world/random_events.gd), EventProps (scripts/art/event_props.gd), UpdateNotice (scripts/ui/update_notice.gd). Realm "lepre" (Realms.continues_deep). The monster type "flee" and no_count. Net.PROTOCOL = 15 (new message "portal", the vote has "rm").

Tests

  • Unit tests for the events (choice from the seed, the leprechaun flees, is caught or gets away, the portal, the shrine, the fairy ring, the party, the wishing well), Lepreconland (theme, rainbows, the king and the gold, the way on to the next floor, all the way through Main) and the update box. The network test: the leprechaun is caught at the host, the portal appears for both, the team goes through it and on to floor 5.

1.0.1 2026-10-28 — Satan and no checkpoints

Changed

  • Satan, the Devil is Hell's final boss on floor 666 (instead of Natas, who is now Satan's messenger in town: "My master awaits you"). He is just as hard as Jesus — the same life, damage, speed, just as many big attacks, two phases and a "second life" — but fights differently: the fire cage (a ring of hellfire around you with one gap, and the middle burns too), hellfire breath (a cone of fire) and the pentagram. When he falls the first time, he rises in his true form: bigger and faster. New look: black cloak, burning cracks across the chest, a crown of flames and goat horns.

Tests

0.9.6 2026-10-27 — The host hosts the game themselves

Changed

  • No separate server program. The hub server from v0.9.5 has been removed. Whoever chooses *Host* hosts the map in their own game.
  • The game opens the port itself in the host's router (UPnP) when you become host, and finds the host's internet address.
  • Invitation code: the host gets a code like HD-2N1GH-3GR15 (with a *Copy* button) in the Multiplayer window. Friends over the internet type the code into *Join with an invitation code* (an IP address also works). If the router cannot open the port itself, the game says so and explains what the host needs to do.
  • The list shows the open games on your own network — the host's game announces itself, no server. (A list of strangers' games over the internet needs a shared place that all games announce themselves to, so it is not included.)
  • Net.PROTOCOL → hearthdelve-mp-13.

Tests

  • 1282 unit tests (new: invitation codes back and forth, ordinary IP addresses, nonsense is rejected, and that a host without a router gets a message instead of an error). The net test: the client finds the host's game in the list (announced by the host itself), is rejected with a wrong code and without a code, and gets in with the right one.
  • Not tested: UPnP against a real router (the container has none) and invitation codes over the internet.

0.9.5 2026-10-27 — Multiplayer hub

Added

  • The hub (Esc → Multiplayer, and in the start menu): a list of open games with name, host, number of players, whether the team is on a delve, whether the game has a password, and whether it is a different version. Press *Join*. Games on your own network show up by themselves (LAN). Games over the internet are shown when a hub server has been set up.
  • As host you give your game a name and can set a password. Without the right password you cannot get in ("Wrong password." / "The game is locked with a password."). The password is sent as a hash, never as text, and the hub server never gets to see it.
  • The hub server (hub-server/hub_server.py in the package, see HUB_SERVER.md): a small Python program with no dependencies, which must run on a machine that is switched on and has UDP port 24567 open. It only keeps the list; the game still connects directly to the host (port 24565 must be open on the host's side).
  • You can still type in an IP address directly (now also with a password).

Changed

  • Net.PROTOCOL → hearthdelve-mp-12.

Tests

  • 1276 unit tests (new: a game is heard on the network, the password is sent as a hash). The hub server's own test (--selftest). The net test now starts a hub server: the host announces its game with a name and password, the client finds it in the list, is rejected with a wrong password and without one, and gets in with the right one.
  • Not tested: the hub server on a real server on the internet, and the LAN list on a real network with several computers (tested on one machine).

0.9.4 2026-10-27 — Fog of war

Added

  • Fog of war: rooms you have not been inside are covered in darkness. You cannot see the floor, monsters, torches, chests, prisoners or the stairs on the other side of the walls. When you enter a room, the darkness lifts (it fades away). Monsters in the darkness are invisible, including their health bars.
  • The minimap now only shows the rooms you have been in (before, you could glimpse the rooms behind the doors).

Tests

  • Known limitation: at the northern edge of a dark room the darkness lies low (otherwise it would cover the hero when he stands by the wall below), so you can make out the top of the wall there.

0.9.3 2026-10-27 — Map, relics and no escaping death

Added

  • Minimap in the bottom right corner in dungeons. It shows the rooms you have been in (and those you have glimpsed through a door), the doors, you (a gold arrow), your fellow players (blue), the settlers following you (green) and the stairs, once you have seen them. In the Well's pitch-black cave, the exit is only shown once you have found it. M gives a large map in the middle of the screen.

An unholy one gives lots of damage, life drain and critical damage — and horns and dark red demon wings. The wings beat slowly (faster when you run), and fellow players can see them. You can only wear one relic at a time.

Changed

  • Legendary items are more than 3 times rarer, and set items are about 3 times rarer (also from bosses).
  • Net.PROTOCOL → hearthdelve-mp-11 (relics are shown to fellow players).

Fixed

  • Monsters could cause an error message if their target disappeared at the same moment (e.g. on a scene change).

Tests

  • 1267 unit tests (new: the minimap, the countdown and the death, the relics and the one-relic rule). The net test is green (69 + 11). Screenshots checked: the map (small and large), the countdown, the halo and angel wings, the horns and demon wings.
  • Not tested: on Windows, over the internet, and the update against GitHub itself.

0.9.2 2026-10-27 — New features and bug fixes

Fixed

  • The host could not free prisoners in co-op. The host's own click switched the cage off before the host itself checked it, so the host thought it had already been opened. Now the host remembers opened cages separately.

0.9.1 2026-10-27 — Automatic updates

Added

  • The game updates itself: on startup it fetches latest.json from its GitHub repository (Hearthdelve_Windows / Hearthdelve_Linux). If there is a newer version, "New version ... is ready" is shown in the top right corner of the start screen. *Update now* downloads the package, checks the checksum (sha256), puts the new program file in place, saves and restarts. The old file is called *.old until the next start.
  • tools/make_release.sh <mappe> builds both packages and their latest.json.
  • The version is shown in the bottom right corner of the start screen (Updater.VERSION).

Changed

  • The Linux package is now a .zip (like Windows), so the game can unpack it itself.

Requirements

  • The two repositories must be public (the game does not log in to GitHub).
  • The game must be in a folder the user owns (not "Program Files"), otherwise it cannot replace itself.
  • Players with v0.9.0 must download v0.9.1 manually once (v0.9.0 has no built-in updating).

Tests

  • 1196 unit tests. The whole update has been tested against a local web server with a fake program file: a new version is found, downloaded, the checksum is checked, the file is replaced, the old one is cleaned up at the next start, and a damaged file is rejected.
