Welcome to Hearthdelve

Build your town above the Deep, dive for gold, materials and gear, and come home alive. Die down there and you lose everything you carry — only the chest in town is safe. Look everything up here: monsters, drop chances, where items are found, recipes and the town's buildings.

The game is at version 1.6.4. See what is new in the changelog.

Classes and skills

Every hero has a class with three branches in the skill tree. The class's favourite weapons give +20% damage and +10% attack speed.

Subclasses (rebirth / prestige)

When you have beaten Hell (holy subclass) or the Heavenly Stairway (unholy), the hero can be reborn: level 1, everything in the Legacy chest, no gold, the blood pact is broken — but with a subclass, a new branch, a skill on key 5 and +5% damage and +5% life for every rebirth. From then on the hero can only use gear of its own prestige level ("Prestige N" items).

Monsters

Monsters are as strong as your hero + 10% (life, damage per hit and armour) — half of that on floor 1, full strength from floor 10. Every kind has its own strength compared with the others (the "Strength" column: 1 = an average monster). Elite monsters have 2.6x life and 1.4x damage. They attack 20% faster for every floor (at most 4 times as fast).

Floors, themes and realms

Every floor has a theme (The Crypt, The Troll Cave, The Sunken Fortress, The Ash Forge). Rooms are 20 x 20 cells. You can only move on when 80% of the monsters are dead and the guardian (every 5th floor) has fallen.

Special floor Chance per floor What is there
The Gold Chamber 1.2% Extra chests and piles of gold, a treasure guardian, 6x chance of named items.
The Rainbow Cave 0.7% Crystal creatures, more elixirs and collectibles, the rainbow elixir, 6x chance of named items.

Random events

About 30% of the ordinary floors (from floor 2) have an event:

Event Share (when there is one) Appears What happens
The Leprechaun 16% from floor 3 Catch him before he vanishes through the walls — then he leaves gold and a portal to Lepreconland (the Leprechaun King and his pot of gold: 60 gold x the floor).
The old shrine 26% from floor 2 A blessing (damage, full life/lantern or luck) or a curse.
The fairy ring 23% from floor 2 Step in: giant, tiny, fast, mirrored, rainbow-coloured — or moved.
The goblin party 19% from floor 3 Five goblins; the ale barrel makes you drunk and four times as strong.
The wishing well 16% from floor 2 Throw in 10 x the floor in gold: 4x the gold, a gift, healing — or nothing.

The floor's challenge

About 40% of the floors from floor 2 have either a pack of elite monsters (3-5) or a champion: one giant monster with 8x life, 2x damage and 6x experience, which leaves two good items and gold.

Set items and named items drop from floor 3 (or on special floors), relics from floor 6. A room has a treasure chest with 70% chance. Spore mushrooms and magma slimes drop a drink/mushroom in 30% of the kills. Champions always leave two good items.

The guardian's hoard

A guardian always leaves a new recipe (if there are more to learn at that depth — see Recipes), 4-7 of every common material, 2-4 embers, gold and gear.

The workshop

Upgrade (+quality, up to +3, +5 with a smith in the Forge), enchant (more properties), reforge (to your hero's level) and salvage (materials back).

Materials are carried home from the Deep (they are lost if you die) and stored in town. Elite monsters drop double, and there is one more for every 6 floors.

The blood pact

At the Sacrificial Stone the town can sacrifice settlers (up to 10): monsters get stronger (+12% life, +7% damage per level), but you find more materials and better items. At the highest pact the Well awakens. The pact breaks after the Well and on rebirth.

Good to know

Topic Rule
Death You lose everything you carry (backpack, gold and materials). The chest in town is safe.
Gear level Items above your hero's level only work with hero level / item level of their power (e.g. level 5 with a level 20 item: 25%).
Prestige items A reborn hero can only use gear of its own prestige level. Everyone can use drinks and collectibles.
Pets Rats and skeletons get 15% of the hero's life, damage and armour on top of the skill's numbers.
